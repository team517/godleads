No. Connect as many as you want on any plan. Pricing follows send volume and AI Agent replies, so adding senders to spread that volume costs nothing.

Two to three weeks for a fresh domain. Warmup keeps running once campaigns start, and volume ramps on its own as reputation builds.

Can I bring my own domains and IPs?+

Yes. Existing domains connect in a few minutes, and client workspaces and white label are available from the Growth plan upward.

Does it work for agencies with many clients?+

That is most of our base. From the Growth plan you can create a workspace per client and white label the whole thing under your own brand.
